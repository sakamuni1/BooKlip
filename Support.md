# BooKlip Support

BooKlip is a macOS productivity app for capturing and organizing clipboard items, files, screenshots, voice memos, and notes.

## Get help

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue in the [BooKlip GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/sakamuni1/BooKlip/issues).

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Your BooKlip version
- Your macOS version
- The steps that led to the problem
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead

Please do not include passwords, private clipboard contents, or other sensitive information in an issue. Remove or redact any sensitive details from screenshots and logs before sharing them.

## Billing and purchases

App Store billing, refunds, and payment methods are managed by Apple. For purchase or refund assistance, visit [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing).
